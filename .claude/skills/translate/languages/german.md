# German

- **Salutation and sign-off** follow German convention: "Dear X Team" → "Liebes X-Team" (or "Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren" when the source is formal and addresses no one), "Sincerely" → "Mit freundlichen Grüßen".
- **Address the reader** with the formal "Sie".
- **Percent:** a thin space before the sign, `17\,\%` in LaTeX and `17 %` in Markdown.
- **Numbers:** decimal comma (`1.5` → `1,5`) and a period for thousands (`10,000` → `10.000`); `110k+` stays as is.
- **Compound nouns** mixing an English and a German term join with hyphens: `Machine-Learning-Pipeline`, `Python-Entwickler`.
